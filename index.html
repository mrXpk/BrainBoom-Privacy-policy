Welcome to BrainBoom. This Privacy Policy is published by Pratap Builds ("we", "our", or "us") to inform players of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play the BrainBoom mobile application across Android devices.

By downloading, installing, or playing BrainBoom, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use our application.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

BrainBoom is designed with user privacy at its core. We do not require account registration, social media logins, email addresses, phone numbers, or real names to play. Specifically:

  • We do not collect or store your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • We do not require or maintain user passwords or user profiles on remote servers.
  • We do not access your contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, or photo library.
  • We do not collect precise GPS location data.

2. Locally Stored Game Data

To maintain your puzzle progress, BrainBoom stores operational gameplay variables locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences storage:

  • Progress & Levels: Current level index, completed levels (Levels 1–50), and brain score/IQ rankings.
  • Virtual Economy: In-game virtual gold coins, unlocked mascot outfits, and victory multipliers. (Virtual coins have no real-world monetary value).
  • Game Settings: Audio preferences (music volume, sound effects toggle, haptics toggle), daily login streak counts, and quickstart completion status.

💡 Player Control: You can completely erase all locally stored game data and reset your progress at any time by selecting the "Restart All Progress" option within the in-app Settings menu, or by clearing app storage in your Android device settings.

3. Device Hardware Sensors (Accelerometer)

Certain interactive levels within BrainBoom utilize your device's built-in hardware accelerometer sensor to detect device tilt, gentle shaking, or orientation changes to solve physical puzzles.

  • Sensor data is processed strictly in real-time in memory on your physical device.
  • Sensor readings are never recorded, logged, saved to disk, or transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

BrainBoom is an ad-supported game. We integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to serve advertisements, including banner ads, interstitial breaks between puzzle stages, and optional rewarded video ads (such as watching an ad to earn bonus hints or virtual coins).

To display and measure relevant ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device and usage data in compliance with Google's privacy standards:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), IP address, and network provider.
  • Device Specs: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Ad impressions, clicks, video completion rates, and crash/diagnostic logs for fraud prevention.

For more information on how Google collects and processes advertising data, please review Google's official policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Your Privacy Controls & Ad Choices

You have full control over advertising identifiers on Android:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete Advertising ID: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID to prevent apps from viewing your personalized ad identifier.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Toggle off personalized advertising in your Google Account ad settings.
